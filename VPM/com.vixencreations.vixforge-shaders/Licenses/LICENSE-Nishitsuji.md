Toon, Clothing Pro and Fur Pro. Its distance field and turbulence are adapted
from a shader Yohei Nishitsuji (@YoheiNishitsuji) published on X on 2026-08-29,
at x.com/YoheiNishitsuji/status/2093826019346301256. The surrounding effect, the
Weave style and every control are our own.
