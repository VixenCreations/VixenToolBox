# Unity Built-in Shaders

Unity's built-in shader source is released under the MIT license below. The VixForge shaders started as the output
of Unity's surface shader compiler, and parts of their passes keep its structure (the tessellation control points
and the pass setup). `vw_GILightOnly` in `VixForgeLighting.cginc` follows `UnityGI_Base` from
`UnityGlobalIllumination.cginc`.
