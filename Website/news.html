Shaders Ascend to crazy new heights: Showcasing the raw beauty of Unity

Shader Update Latex Ultra VixForge Toon August 7, 2026

The Detail Update - Latex Ultra 4.3.0 and Toon 1.2.0

This one is all about fine detail. The texture you painted comes back out of the surface, there are two brand-new Special Effects, and there is a pile of quality-of-life work underneath. Everything new is off by default, so your existing materials look exactly as they did until you reach for it.

Detail That Actually Reads

Your maps do more with the light now, and most of it needs no new textures to bake.

  • Cavity sharpens the fine detail already in your height channel. Pores, seams and stitching sink into shadow, ridges catch the light. It reads the Packed PBR Mask you already have.
  • Micro Shading and Micro Lighting make your occlusion react to where the light comes from - creases go dark under a side light, raised detail brightens - instead of just dimming everything evenly. Each has a trim per lighting system.
  • Bent Normals let ambient light follow the shape of the surface, so crevices stay dark and forms read solid. Drop in a bent normal map baked next to your AO.

Your Maps, Made Easier

  • A Height Map slot of its own, so Substance and Marmoset height exports go straight in instead of being repacked by hand. It drives Parallax, Displacement and Cavity.
  • Auto on every mask channel. Masks read black-and-white and alpha-carried images without you working out which is which. Set a channel to Auto if a mask ever comes out blank.
  • Detail Strength works without a Micro Detail Map now, sharpening the fine detail already sitting in your normal map. Pores and skin grain, with no second texture.

Two New Special Effects

  • Infinity Mirror. The surface reads as glass over a light that reflects away into the distance, a receding tunnel that shifts as you move around it. Pick a ring of lights, a dot grid, or your own pattern.
  • Inner Cosmos. A window onto a living galaxy with real depth: look in from any angle and it parallaxes and swirls, and it never repeats. Fully procedural, no texture needed. Turn on Living Energy with AudioLink in the world and the whole volume moves to the music.

And More to Play With

  • Flipbooks - an animated overlay from a GIF, an image sequence or a sprite sheet, placed on the mesh like a decal.
  • Car Paint - a pearlescent flip-flop that travels with your view, with metallic flake and a wet clearcoat gloss.
  • Transparency slider - fades the whole material out in the Fade and Transparent modes.
  • Toon gets a gradient editor - build the shading ramp right in the inspector, no external texture needed.

Full notes, including the fixes and a couple of "heads up" items for existing materials, are on the shader changelogs.

Vixens Toolbox Milestone: Over 1,500 Downloads and Counting

Milestone Thank You July 10, 2026

Thank You for 1,500+ Downloads

It's official: the Vixens Toolbox has crossed 1,500 downloads. Every single one of those is a creator who trusted our tools with their avatars and their worlds, and we could not be more grateful.

When VixForge Interactive started, the goal was simple: kill the repetitive, mind-numbing parts of the VRChat pipeline so creators could spend their time actually creating. Fifteen hundred downloads later, that mission is resonating far beyond what we ever imagined.

What 1,500+ Means to Us

  • Every download is a creator we get to build for, and a reason to keep raising the bar.
  • Real QEM mesh decimation, non-destructive Quest conversion, multi-ecosystem world auditing, and the VixenWear Latex Ultra shader, all shipping in one free VPM package.
  • A growing community that files issues, shares screenshots, and pushes these tools to their limits.

To every creator who installed the toolbox, reported a bug, requested a feature, or simply told a friend: thank you. You are the reason this project keeps growing, and we are only just getting started.

Here's to the next 1,500. Onward.
