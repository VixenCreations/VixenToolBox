Milestone Thank You July 10, 2026

Thank You for 1,500+ Downloads

It's official: the Vixens Toolbox has crossed 1,500 downloads. Every single one of those is a creator who trusted our tools with their avatars and their worlds, and we could not be more grateful.

When VixForge Interactive started, the goal was simple: kill the repetitive, mind-numbing parts of the VRChat pipeline so creators could spend their time actually creating. Fifteen hundred downloads later, that mission is resonating far beyond what we ever imagined.

What 1,500+ Means to Us

  • Every download is a creator we get to build for, and a reason to keep raising the bar.
  • Real QEM mesh decimation, non-destructive Quest conversion, multi-ecosystem world auditing, and the VixenWear Latex Ultra shader, all shipping in one free VPM package.
  • A growing community that files issues, shares screenshots, and pushes these tools to their limits.

To every creator who installed the toolbox, reported a bug, requested a feature, or simply told a friend: thank you. You are the reason this project keeps growing, and we are only just getting started.

Here's to the next 1,500. Onward.

VixForge Interactive x Enigma Industries: Forging Realities. Building Experiences.

Partnership Studio News July 4, 2026

VixForge Interactive and Enigma Industries are joining forces. Two studios, two strengths, one shared goal: giving VRChat creators better tools, stronger communities, and bigger opportunities to bring their ideas to life. This is the first step in a much larger vision, and it starts now.

Two Studios, One Vision

For years you knew them as Club Enigma, a community built around events and bringing people together. That vision grew into something far larger: building worlds, experimenting with new technology, exploring game development, and backing projects that reach well beyond the dance floor. Today that next step has a new name: Enigma Industries, a studio committed to innovation, creativity, and shaping the future of social VR.

VixForge Interactive is recognized across the VRChat creator community for its Unity development and the Vixens Toolbox, our comprehensive Unity VRChat creator suite. It is built to simplify development, streamline workflows, assist with optimization, and automate the repetitive work, so creators spend more time building and less time managing technical overhead.

What Enigma Industries Brings

Enigma Industries brings years of building community and unforgettable moments in VRChat. What began with events and bringing people together has grown into a studio focused on the experiences themselves:

  • Immersive Worlds - ambitious, hand-crafted VRChat spaces designed to be lived in, not just visited.
  • Live Events and Community - the heartbeat of Club Enigma, now scaled up: shows, gatherings, and a community that shows up.
  • Innovative Experiences - new technology, game development, and projects that push what social VR can be.
  • A Bigger Vision - not just a rebrand, but the first step in a much larger transformation for social VR.

What the Partnership Delivers

While VixForge Interactive continues empowering creators through advanced Unity tooling, Enigma Industries will keep focusing on immersive worlds, innovative experiences, and ambitious projects that push the boundaries of what is possible in VRChat. This partnership is about more than what we are building today:

  • It's about sharing knowledge.
  • It's about inspiring innovation.
  • It's about supporting creators.
  • It's about helping shape the next generation of social VR.

This Is Just the Beginning

Together, we're laying the foundation for a future where creators have better tools, stronger communities, and even greater opportunities to bring their ideas to life. This is the first step in a much larger transformation, and it's where the next chapter begins.

The future starts now. Enigma Industries x VixForge Interactive.
