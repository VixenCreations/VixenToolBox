About VixForge Interactive

Forging Realities. Building Experiences.

We are an independent creator-tools studio building professional-grade software for VRChat: shaders, avatar and world tooling, and Unity editor automation that help creators produce high-fidelity content faster and with less friction.

Who We Are

VixForge Interactive is the studio behind the Vixens Toolbox, our professional-grade VRChat package suite. Our work targets Unity 2022.3 on the Built-In Render Pipeline, the active standard for VRChat projects, and ships through the VRChat Package Manager (VPM). We focus on the technical heavy lifting so creators can spend more time building and less time managing overhead.

Flagship: Vixens Toolbox

The Vixens Toolbox delivers high-fidelity materials, PhysBone automation pipelines, and Unity editor utilities in one integrated package. Highlights include:

  • VixenWear Latex Ultra - our flagship latex shader, with a dedicated Latex Ultra SPS variant, plus dynamic PBR systems for fur, cloth, and nylon.
  • Vixen Hub - the unified editor hub that ties the suite together.
  • Avatar tooling - validation, mesh patching, Quest conversion, and PhysBone automation.
  • Vixen World Engine - scene and world building utilities.
  • Vixen Badge Studio and Magick.NET-based texture pipelines for optimized image and material workflows.

Extended Projects and R&D

Beyond the shipping toolbox, we invest in R&D for next-generation creator workflows: UdonSharp and C# frameworks, spatial engine experiments (VixenLens on Rust and Tauri), automation pipelines, and prototype editor systems that feed back into our released tools. Projects like Surface Ultra, ClothingPro, and VixForge Director are actively in development.
