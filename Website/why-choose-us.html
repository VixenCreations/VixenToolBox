Why use the toolbox

What the Vixens Toolbox does for you, next to what you would do without it. Where another tool does the job well, we say so.

Mesh reduction inside Unity

Vixens Toolbox

Brings heavy meshes down to your triangle target with the same kind of reduction as Blender's Decimate, without leaving Unity. Eyes, face and hands are protected, and every blendshape is rebuilt so visemes keep working.

Without it

Decimate in Blender and re-export, or buy a Unity plugin, then check that your visemes still work.

Texture memory you can trust

Vixens Toolbox

Follows Animator controllers, VRCFury and animation clips to find every texture your avatar can load. Compression is offered before resizing, so you save memory without the blur.

Without it

Textures that only load through a toggle or an animation are easy to miss when you add up memory by hand.

Quest conversion

Quest Conversion Engine

Works on a copy, locks out the components Quest rejects, moves every material to the mobile shader you pick, and shows every Quest limit for your rank in one panel before you convert.

VRCQuestTools and d4rk

Both are strong, and shaped differently. VRCQuestTools bakes each supported shader's look into Quest textures, with dedicated Poiyomi and lilToon generators, through NDMF. d4rk runs on upload and specialises in merging meshes, materials and blend shapes.

Reusable PhysBone setups

PhysBone Blueprints

Saves a whole PhysBone setup, colliders included, and puts it back on another version of the same avatar.

VRCFury

Adds and edits PhysBones non-destructively, but cannot save a whole setup and re-apply it to another avatar.

Avatar cleanup you can review

Optimization Suite

One scan lists unused empty objects, switched-off components, unused end bones, oversized bounds and heavy meshes as tasks. Nothing runs until you tick it.

d4rk and Thry's

d4rk works on upload and excels at merging meshes, materials and blend shapes. Thry's shader optimizer bakes material settings into the shader. Both go deep in their own area.

World checks in one scan

Vixen World Engine

More than 160 checks across ten packages, including ProTV, TXL, VizVid, IwaSync3, AudioLink, LTCGI and VRC Light Volumes. Most findings come with a one-click fix.

Without it

Each package is checked by hand, in its own inspector.

MMD dances

VixenMMD

Builds the MMD face shapes dance worlds use from your face tracking and visemes, checks a dance for shapes you are missing, and reads Make It MMD's presets.

Make It MMD

Did the same job and is no longer updated. VixenMMD converts its setup in one click.

Animation curves

Animation Workbench Pro

Builds smooth curves from exact values, keys any material or component property, and plays them on your avatar before you commit.

Unity's Animation window

Good for keying by hand. Building an eased curve between two exact values is up to you.

Convention badges

Vixen Badge Studio

Renders your name and title onto a convention badge, fills in the material, and lets you place text by clicking the badge in the Scene view. Furality layouts are included.

Without it

Text is placed by hand in an image editor, and the material is rebuilt for each event.

Latex and synthetic materials

VixenWear Latex Ultra (sold separately)

Our own shader for latex and other synthetic materials: clearcoat, thin-film, stretch highlights, wet and drip effects, and live reactions to AudioLink, LTCGI, VRC Light Volumes and VRSL. Works with VRCFury SPS.

Poiyomi and lilToon

Mature, general-purpose shaders and the right default for most avatars. They are not made for latex, so that look takes extra modules and hand-tuning.

The bottom line

Vixens Toolbox

Free through VPM and GitHub. It is newer, so its community is smaller, and the texture tools need ImageMagick, which runs in the Windows and Linux editors.

A mix of tools

Also free, and each one excellent at its job. You put the chain together and keep it working yourself.
