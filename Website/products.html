Our Products

Everything shipping today, and where to pick each one up. All four shaders share the same lighting stack and the same toolkit, so whatever you learn on one carries straight over to the others. The Vixens Toolbox itself is free on the VPM Registry page.

VixenWear Latex Ultra

A dual-lobe GGX shader for synthetic materials: clearcoat, thin-film, wet and drip, melting goo, triple matcaps, and live reactions to AudioLink, LTCGI, VRC Light Volumes and VRSL. Ships with a tessellation-free SPS twin for VRCFury.

VixForge Toon

A cel and anime shader carrying the same toolkit, with rebuilt outlines. Coming from lilToon, Poiyomi or VRChat's Toon Standard, the inspector translates your existing materials across in one click, and can convert a whole avatar at once with a backup and full undo.

VixenWear Clothing Pro

The fabric companion to Latex Ultra. A soft sheen so silk, satin and velvet catch the light at grazing angles, surface fur for wool and fleece, real fur grown as geometry, and a Material Type picker with 72 fabrics that sets a sensible starting point in one click.

VixForge Fur Pro

Real fur, grown as geometry rather than painted on. Every hair is one continuous strand from root to tip, with self shadowing, guard hairs and tufting, and a brush that lets you comb the coat straight in the scene view. Clothing Pro with the cloth half taken out and the fur turned up.
