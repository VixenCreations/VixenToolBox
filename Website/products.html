Questions

Before You Grab One

Do these shaders work on Quest or Android?

No. They are for VRChat on desktop PC only. VRChat replaces any non-mobile shader with its own on Quest and Android, so use one of VRChat's mobile shaders for those builds.

Which Unity version and render pipeline do I need?

Unity 2022.3 and the Built-in Render Pipeline, which is what VRChat uses. They are not built for URP or HDRP.

Do they work with VRCFury and Modular Avatar?

Yes. Materials lock automatically when you press the VRChat SDK build button, and that pass runs after VRCFury has finished building, so anything VRCFury generates gets covered too.

Do I have to lock my materials before I upload?

No, it happens for you on upload. You can also lock or unlock a whole avatar at any time from the Hub.

My mesh renders as shredded triangles. What happened?

The model was imported with Keep Quads switched on. These shaders tessellate, and tessellation needs triangles. Run VixenTools > VixForge Mesh Fixes > Scan for Quad Meshes, and it will find the meshes and fix them.

Can I use these on avatars I sell?

Latex Ultra and VixForge Toon, yes, commercial work included. Leave the copyright headers in the files and do not resell the package on its own. Clothing Pro and Fur Pro are supporter-only and may not be redistributed.

What do they cost?

Latex Ultra and VixForge Toon are free on Gumroad. Clothing Pro and Fur Pro come with a Patreon membership, at $35 and $15 a month.
