# VixForge Interactive x Enigma Industries

## Strategic Partnership Announcement

**Forging Realities. Building Experiences.**
Together, we're redefining immersive creation.

---

## The Headline

VixForge Interactive and Enigma Industries are joining forces. Two studios, two strengths, one shared goal: giving VRChat creators better tools, stronger communities, and bigger opportunities to bring their ideas to life.

This is the first step in a much larger vision, and it starts now.

## Two Studios, One Vision

For years you knew them as **Club Enigma**, a community built around events and bringing people together. That vision grew into something far larger: building worlds, experimenting with new technology, exploring game development, and backing projects that reach well beyond the dance floor. Today that next step has a new name: **Enigma Industries**, a studio committed to innovation, creativity, and shaping the future of social VR.

**VixForge Interactive** is recognized across the VRChat creator community for its Unity development and the **Vixens Toolbox**, our comprehensive Unity VRChat creator suite. It is built to simplify development, streamline workflows, assist with optimization, and automate the repetitive work, so creators spend more time building and less time managing technical overhead.

Two different strengths. One shared goal.

## What the Partnership Delivers

- **VixForge Interactive** keeps empowering creators with advanced Unity tooling, shaders, and automation.
- **Enigma Industries** keeps pushing immersive worlds, innovative experiences, and ambitious projects that expand what is possible in VRChat.
- **Together** we share knowledge, inspire innovation, support creators, and help shape the next generation of social VR.

## This Is Just the Beginning

This partnership is about more than what we are shipping today. It is the foundation for a future where creators have better tools, stronger communities, and greater room to turn ideas into reality.

The future starts now.

**Enigma Industries x VixForge Interactive**
