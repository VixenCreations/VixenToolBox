Shader Features

In progressShader Features

Dissolve

The dissolve itself is done and on the Upcoming Changes page: your material can fade, burn or break away, with its own shape, edge line and glow, and the shadow, the outline and fur go with it. Now we're going further than the usual dissolve:

  • the edge can shatter into flying shards;
  • the dissolved area can open onto Hyperspace or Inner Cosmos, so your body becomes a window;
  • the beat can run the dissolve across your body, and Cymatics patterns can shape it;
  • your material can melt away with the goo as it goes;
  • it can look different in mirrors, on camera and to other players;
  • getting close can open a window into the body;
  • the cut can show a solid inside instead of a hollow shell;
  • glitter can spark along the edge.

Worlds And The Light Baker

LaterWorlds And The Light Baker

Light Baker 1.0

The first release of the Light Baker as its own product: bake a world's lighting on your graphics card, in one step, into Unity's own lightmaps, light probes and Light Volumes.

LaterWorlds And The Light Baker

Light probes straight from the baker

Place and bake light probes with the Light Baker alone, with no trip through Unity's lightmapper first.

LaterWorlds And The Light Baker

More of your Lighting settings

The baker will follow more of the settings in Unity's Lighting window, such as sample counts and lightmap compression, so a scene set up for Unity's lightmapper bakes the same way here.

LaterWorlds And The Light Baker

Sharper baked lighting on normal maps

A new lightmap option for scenes lit with the VixForge world shaders that keeps normal map detail in baked light at a smaller file size.

LaterWorlds And The Light Baker

Height-blended layers for World Surface

Paint where materials meet with vertex colour, and let them blend by height, so moss settles into the gaps between stones instead of fading over them.

LaterWorlds And The Light Baker

Windows with rooms behind them

Interior mapping for World Surface: a flat window shows a room with depth that shifts as you walk past.
