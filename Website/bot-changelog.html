VixForge Bot 1.18.0 - 2026-11-20

The fox gets the jokes now: memes, knock-knock jokes, table flips, and thousands of new things to say.

Added

  • Speaks meme. Say F, rip, 💀, W, L, gg, bruh, sus, based, cringe, sheesh, oof, stonks, touch grass, no cap, it's giving, big brain, smol, monke, this is fine, +1000 aura, NPC, yapping or "it do be like that" to the fox and it plays along. Say "rip" right after something sad, though, and it comforts you instead of joking.
  • Table flips. Flip a table (╯°□°)╯︵ ┻━┻ and the fox puts it back, even if you didn't ping it. Keep flipping and it starts counting. Put one back yourself and the fox thanks you for it.
  • Knock-knock jokes. Say "knock knock" and the fox plays along: who's there, who, then a laugh or a groan. You don't need to ping it while the joke's going. Ask it to "tell me a knock knock joke" and it knocks on your door instead.
  • Memes on request. "Tell me a meme" gets a joke from the new Memes category, which is in /joke too.
  • Narration. Say "narrate this" and the fox describes the chat like a nature documentary or a movie trailer.
  • Hype. Slay, serve, iconic, swag or "let's gooo" at the fox gets full hype back.
  • Sympathy when things break. Tell the fox your PC died or your files got wiped and it's sorry with you, plus a recovery tip when it's lost data.
  • TMI. Bathroom stories get a "TMI! covers ears" in public channels.
  • A friend in your DMs. The fox now remembers what you tell it in DMs, like your name, your pets and your plans, and checks in later: how did the exam go, how's your dog. It only keeps your recent messages, how you've been feeling, or health plans like a doctor's appointment if you say yes: it asks the first time you tell it you're having a hard time, and /memory moods changes your answer. It's encrypted, never shown to anyone else, and deleted after 90 days of quiet. /memory show shows what it remembers, /memory forget wipes it and /memory off stops it, or just say "forget me".
  • Therapy pet mode. Having a hard time? Tell the fox in DMs and it switches to gentle mode: it listens, reflects how you're feeling, offers calming tricks like box breathing and grounding, and skips the jokes until you say you feel better. It's a bot, not a therapist. For things like abuse, eating struggles, substance use, trauma or medication questions, it gently points you to free, confidential help on findahelpline.com, and to emergency services if you're in danger. The crisis line picker is still one tap away.
  • Your secrets stay secret. The fox never shares what anyone has told it. Ask it what someone else said and you get a polite no.
  • A quiet offer. Say you're sad in the server and the fox now offers to talk it through privately in DMs.
  • A look when it's lost. A short reply the fox doesn't understand, to something it said in the last 15 minutes, gets a 👀 instead of silence.
  • Much more to say. Over 200 new lines for every pet command, every play game and every way you can answer the fox ("same", "really?", "why?", "wow", "no u", "idk", "love you", "whatever" and more). Tell it you're annoyed and it grumbles along with you, then helps you cool down.
  • More about the fox. Over 200 new answers for each question about the fox itself: its favourite animal, colour, emoji, game, holiday, movie, song, season, toy and treats, its star sign, crush, day, dreams, job, talents and superpower, what makes it laugh, what scares it and what's on its mind. Ask whether it's a furry, what species it is, what it's coded in, whether it can hear you in voice, where it lives and sleeps, what it looks like, whether it has pets, or whether it's single, ticklish, rich, smart, lonely, okay or a night owl. Ask it for a secret, a story, the news, its weekend plans or the meaning of life, whether you can trust it, and whether it remembers you, misses you or likes you (it does). Ask if it plays VRChat: it can't, but it wants to see your photos.

Fixed

  • "I wiped my hard drive" got a chat about Unity instead of sympathy. The fox is sorry now, and has tips.
  • "Gay ass" and "Table flip" sent as replies to the fox got no answer at all.
  • Slay talk at the fox got a random reply.
  • Telling the fox it was gonna fall down a well got a chat about autumn. It now answers "careful" and "watch out" with its own lines, and only talks seasons when you do.

The fox tells stories now. Real ones, made up on the spot, never the same twice.

  • Three new joke kinds. Foxes and furries, space, and gaming and VR, with 200 new jokes across all kinds.
  • Lots more to say. Over 200 new riddles, fortunes, shower thoughts, compliments, tongue twisters, conversation topics and would you rathers each.
  • More roasts. About 70 new burns across mild, spicy and nuclear, plus new ways to ask: "cook me", "humble me", "diss me", "destroy me", and "gloves off" or "show no mercy" for nuclear.
  • The fox roasts itself. Tell it "roast yourself" and it turns the heat on its own fluffy tail.
