VixForge Bot Changelog

VixForge Bot 1.9.0 - 2026-11-17

Staff get real tools: moderation commands, a server log, events, giveaways, announcements and polls.

Added

  • /mod. Warn, time out, kick, ban and unban with a reason the member gets by DM. /mod history shows a member's warnings and actions from the last 90 days.
  • Channel controls. /mod purge clears recent messages, /mod slowmode slows a channel down, and /mod lock closes it until /mod unlock.
  • A mod log. Every staff action is posted to a staff channel.
  • /serverlog. A staff-only log of deleted and edited messages, joins, leaves, bans and role changes. Posts are removed after 90 days.
  • Server events. /event create plans an event, the bot announces it, pings interested members before it starts, and can start and end it on time.
  • Giveaways. /giveaway start posts a giveaway with an Enter button and draws the winners when time runs out. End early, reroll or cancel any time.
  • /announce posts a formatted announcement as the bot, and /poll starts a Discord poll.
VixForge Bot 1.8.0 - 2026-11-17

You can DM the bot now, and it keeps a conversation going instead of going quiet.

Added

  • DM the bot. Members of the server can chat with the bot in DMs, the same way as pinging it in a channel.
  • "Again", "another one" and "one more" get you another line of whatever the bot just said, like another story or another trick.
  • The bot answers its own questions' answers. Say "good", "meh" or "hbu" after it asks how you are, and it replies properly.
  • No more silent replies. Reply to the bot with a full sentence and it answers, even when it has nothing specific to say.
  • More things to talk about: ask if it can hear you in voice, what its job is, its star sign or the meaning of life. Tell it you're at the gym, going on a date, on vacation or moving. Bring up the seasons, sports, books, tabletop games, cars, plushies or your favourite species.

Fixed

  • "Isn't that right?" gets the bot backing you up instead of an 8-ball answer.
  • "Zoomies?" and "Zoom!" start the zoomies.
  • "Talk dirty to me" and "suck it" get an answer when you reply to the bot.
VixForge Bot 1.7.0 - 2026-11-17

The bot chats about your day, gets the zoomies, and has a lot more to say in voice.

Added

  • Small talk. Tell the bot what you're up to (playing a game, eating, coding, at work, sick, excited) and it answers, often naming the thing you said. Ask about its life ("wyd", "do you sleep", "tell me a story", "did you miss me") or bring up a topic like Python, Unity, VRChat, avatars, pets or furries, and it has a take.
  • Short replies to the bot like "why", "fr?", "same", "no u" or "rude" get a real answer.
  • Ask the bot to stop. "Stop talking to me" or "go away" makes it leave you alone until you @ it again.
  • Zoomies. Praise the bot enough, or ask it to zoom, and it changes its status, runs through a few busy channels making fox noises, then flops back down. Once an hour at most.
  • Sing, tricks and opinions. Ask the bot to sing, do a trick (sit, roll over, play dead, zoom), or give its opinion on something.
  • Voice: a line when someone goes live, hour marks for long hangouts (with a gentle nudge late at night), a party line when a channel fills up, and a recap when a long hangout ends.
  • /voice hangouts shows which voice channels are busy right now.
  • /voice hide keeps your voice time off /voice top and private to others.
  • /sound random plays a random soundboard sound.
  • Staff can set a join sound the bot plays when someone joins its voice channel, and switch each new voice line on or off.

Changed

  • Hundreds of new lines for praise, comebacks, greetings, "how are you", 8-ball answers, welcomes, pic replies and more. Spamming "good bot" now takes a lot longer to run out of reactions.
  • More ways to praise the bot: "amazing bot", "bestest bot", "goodest bot" and similar now count.
  • The 18+ den has a lot more to say, and its would you rathers are fixed so every one shows up.
VixForge Bot 1.6.0 - 2026-11-07

VixForge Bot 1.4.0 - 2026-11-01

Welcomes, holidays, a live status, and the bot joins voice.

Added

  • Welcomes. New members get a hello by name in the welcome channel, and their first hi gets an answer.
  • Room hellos. Say hi or good morning to the whole room and the bot says it back.
  • Holiday greetings for the big holidays, with matching reactions.
  • A live status that changes through the day and shows things like a new release or how many people are in voice.
  • Voice. /voice join brings the bot into your voice channel to play soundboard sounds with /sound. /voice time and /voice top show time spent in voice. The bot can't hear anyone.
  • Score your own comebacks. Write a burn back at one of the bot's roasts and it rates it out of 10, then fires back.
  • Follow-up reactions when you answer the bot with something short.

Changed

  • The bot knows more small talk and answers more replies to its cards.

VixForge Bot 1.0.0 - 2026-08-23

The first tracked version: GitHub updates in Discord and a guard against scams.

Added

  • GitHub updates. New code, releases, issues and pull requests from our public repositories are posted in a channel.
  • Scam protection. Known scam images, scam links, mass mentions, spam bursts and raids are caught and handled automatically.
  • Single-server lock. The bot only works in our server and leaves any other it gets added to.

Fixed

  • GitHub updates no longer post twice.
