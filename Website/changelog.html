🎨 VixenTools/Latex Suit Ultra: Substance Painter Workflow & Setup

To achieve the intended high-fidelity raymarched depth, clearcoat iridescence, and micro-shadowing, your PBR textures must be packed correctly before importing to Unity.

1. Substance Painter Export Setup (The Packed PBR Map)

The _MetallicGlossMap slot requires a highly specific 4-channel texture packing to optimize VRAM and shader instruction counts:

  • Red Channel (R): Metallic
  • Green Channel (G): Ambient Occlusion (AO)
  • Blue Channel (B): Height / Displacement (Used for Tessellation and Parallax Raymarching)
  • Alpha Channel (A): Smoothness (Inverted Roughness / Glossiness)

How to configure the Export Template in Substance Painter:

  1. Open Export Textures -> Output Templates.
  2. Create a new R+G+B+A texture map. Name it [TextureSet]_PackedPBR.
  3. Drag the Metallic map into the R slot (From Grayscale).
  4. Drag the Mixed AO map into the G slot (From Grayscale).
  5. Drag the Height map into the B slot (From Grayscale).
  6. Drag the Glossiness map into the A slot (From Grayscale). Note: If you use a Roughness workflow, drag Roughness into the A slot and select "Invert".
  7. Save the template and export your textures.

2. Unity Shader Configuration

  • Albedo / Alpha: Slot your Base Color into _MainTex. The Alpha channel controls the _CutOff transparency.
  • Packed PBR: Slot the newly exported [TextureSet]_PackedPBR into the Packed PBR Map slot. Ensure the texture in Unity has sRGB (Color Texture) unchecked (Linear format).
  • Normal Map: Slot your standard DirectX/OpenGL normal map into _BumpMap.

3. Advanced Features Integration

  • Tessellation & Parallax: Adjust the _DisplacementStrength (driven by your B channel) and _Parallax to control physical depth and micro-shadowing.
  • Thin Film Interference: Modify _ThinFilmThickness to shift the iridescent pearlescent color tone.
  • AudioLink: Check Enable AudioLink to dynamically drive Iridescence via the Bass band (_AudioLinkFilmMod) and Emission via the Treble band.
  • LTCGI / Light Volumes: Check the respective toggles to natively hook the latex material into VRChat dynamic lighting ecosystems.
