Haptics Nexus

The dedicated haptic hardware layer of the Stream Connector ecosystem. Haptics Nexus unifies control across multiple device protocols, PiShock, Intiface, OWO and OSC, under a single abstraction, so live events and creators can drive real-world haptics without wrestling a different SDK for every piece of hardware.
