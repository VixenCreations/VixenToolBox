Help Fuel The Forge

Help me rebuild and keep every tool free for the community.

I'm going to be straight with you, because if I'm asking for help you deserve honesty. This year knocked me flat. I was laid off from my job, my accounts went into the negatives, and I had to sell my car just to afford the camper I now live in on my mom's property. I'm rebuilding from the ground up, and the work I pour into these tools is a huge part of how I claw my way back.

Vixens Toolbox never started as a business plan. It started because I was sick of doing the same mind-numbing Unity setups over and over again. It grew into an entire suite, from the Quest Conversion Engine to PhysBone Blueprints, all built to save us hundreds of hours of grinding so we can get back to actually creating. Every bit of it is free, and I am determined to keep it that way.

But keeping this alive is relentless. Reverse-engineering undocumented Unity APIs, fixing the pipeline every time an SDK update breaks it without warning, and keeping the automated VPM distribution running eats an enormous amount of time, time I am fighting to balance against simply getting back on my feet. Your support is what lets me keep doing both.
