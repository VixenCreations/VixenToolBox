VixForge Bot

The forge fox that lives in our Discord.

It chats, plays games, hangs out in voice and keeps the server safe from scams. Type /help in the server to see it all there.

Chat with it

@ the bot, reply to one of its messages, or just say its name. No commands needed.

  • Small talk. Say hi, ask how it's doing, tell it what you're up to (gaming, working on your avatar, suiting up, at a con, can't sleep), or bring up VRChat, pets, birds, memes, school, headsets and plenty more. It always has a take.
  • Ask about it. Its favourite colour, song or game, whether it's a furry, its pronouns, what scares it, what it's coded in. It has an answer for all of it.
  • Quick answers. It can spell a word, count letters, count to 10 (fast, if you ask), do maths with huge numbers, tell you the time or date, pick a number, or settle a "this or that?".
  • "What is...?" Ask about a fandom term, a VRChat term like PhysBones or FBT, or slang like rizz, and it explains.
  • Again! Say "again" or "one more" for another story, joke or trick.
  • Roasts. Ask for a roast and fire back: it scores your comeback out of 10. Battles heat up as they go, and "nuclear roast me" is there if you're brave.
  • Praise and headpats. Call it a good bot and it gets happier and happier, all the way to a full puppy meltdown. Pat it and it pretends not to like it.
  • Spoil it. Give it a treat, pets, a hug, snuggles, belly rubs, scritches, a toy or a tuck-in, or throw a ball for fetch. "*gives treat*" works fine. It counts every treat, and "who gives you the most treats?" shows the top three.
  • Its mood. Treats and pets cheer it up, insults bring it down, and it calms down again over time. Ask "what's your mood?". A grumpy fox gets sassy, a spoiled one goes over the top, and now and then it adds a little fox habit to a reply.
  • Sing, tricks and opinions. Ask it to sing, sit, roll over or play dead, or what it thinks of something.
  • DMs. Server members can chat with it in DMs too.
  • Need a break? Say "stop talking to me" and it leaves you alone until you @ or DM it again.

Is it an AI? No. Every reply is a line we wrote, and the bot picks one that fits.

Games and fun commands

  • Quizzes: /trivia, /factorcap and /riddle, with /scores and a /leaderboard.
  • Party games: /wyr starts a would you rather vote the whole channel can join, plus /truthordare and /8ball.
  • Luck: /coinflip and /roll (like d20 or 2d6).
  • Something to read: /joke, /fact, /topic, /fortune, /showerthought, /tonguetwister and /pickupline.
  • For your friends: /roast, /compliment, /rate anything, /ship two people, or let it /choose for you.
  • Affection: /hug, /pat, /boop, /highfive or /poke someone, and they can send it right back.
  • Arcade: /play hangman, a daily Fox Wordle, word scramble, an emoji quiz, number guessing and a reaction race, or tic-tac-toe, connect four and rock paper scissors against a friend or the fox.
  • Slang: /urban looks up a word on Urban Dictionary for 25 Fox Coins. Crude entries only show in the 18+ den.
  • Maths: ask it to convert units or temperatures, check a prime, write Roman numerals, binary or hex, or work out a percentage.

Fox Coins 🪙

  • Earn: /coins daily and /coins weekly (streaks pay more), a few coins for chatting, /earn work, /earn fish, /earn forage, and wins in trivia and the arcade.
  • Spend: /shop browse for titles, collectables, pet items, and treats and gifts for the fox. Gifts can get the zoomies going sooner. /shop sell turns your catches into coins.
  • Casino: /casino slots, blackjack, coin flip bets and higher or lower. Play money only.
  • Share and show off: /coins give a friend some coins, /coins top for the leaderboard, and /profile for your balance, title and collection.

Adventures and pets

  • Text adventures. /adventure play starts one: pick a class, fight, solve riddles, find treasure and level up. Check your /adventure character and the /adventure leaderboard.
  • Write your own. Supporters can follow /adventure guide and send theirs in with /adventure submit. Staff review it before it goes live.
  • Pets. /pet adopt hatches an egg you get to name. Feed it, play with it, clean up after it and put it to bed, and it grows up. Ignore it and it moves out.
  • Visit a friend's pet with /pet show, and see the oldest pets with /pet top.

Voice

  • Soundboard. /voice join brings the bot into your channel, and /sound plays a soundboard sound (or a random one). It can't hear anyone.
  • It talks. /voice say makes it say what you type in your call, and it reads its own voice-chat lines aloud in the channel it's in.
  • Voice time. /voice time and /voice top show who spends the most time in voice. /voice hide keeps yours private.
  • What's busy? /voice hangouts shows which voice channels have people in them right now.
  • Hangout lines. In a voice channel's chat it says hi when someone opens it, cheers when someone goes live, marks each hour of a long hangout (with a gentle nudge late at night), hypes a full channel and posts a recap when a long hangout ends.

Around the server

  • Welcomes. New members get a hello by name, and the bot answers their first hi and their intro post. Say hi or good morning to the whole room and it says it back.
  • Reactions. It reacts to some messages with emoji, understands custom emoji and stickers by their name, and reacts to every pic in the photo channel.
  • Holidays. Greetings and matching reactions on the big holidays.
  • A live status. It shows what's going on: download counts, the latest version, who's hanging out in voice and new releases as they ship. It naps when chat goes quiet.
  • Zoomies. Praise it enough, or ask it to zoom, and it changes its status, runs through a few busy channels making fox noises, then flops back down. Once an hour at most, and it tells you when they're recharged.
  • Project news. New code, releases, issues and pull requests from our public GitHub repos are posted in a channel, and it celebrates download milestones.
  • Versions. /versions shows the latest version of every product, with links to the full changelogs.
  • Events. New server events get announced. Mark yourself interested and the bot pings you before it starts and again when it goes live.
  • Giveaways. Click Enter on a giveaway post to join, or click again to leave. Winners are drawn when the timer runs out.

The 18+ den

An opt-in corner for adults, only in our age-restricted channels.

  • Run /spicy on in the den for adult jokes, flirts, roasts and dares there. /spicy off turns it back off for you.
  • Can't find the den? Ask the bot how to get into the 18+ section and it walks you through it.
  • Ask the bot why it's so gay in the den and the answer gets spicier. Throw a queer slur at it there and it reclaims it with sass.
  • In the open 18+ photo channel, reply to a pic and ask the bot to rate it, rizz it up or give its opinion.
  • Everywhere else, the bot keeps it all-ages.

PiShock

  • /pishock link connects your own PiShock. Only you see your key.
  • Nobody can use it until you /pishock allow them. Set your own /pishock limits, the /pishock level, and which /pishock modes (shock, vibrate, beep) are on.
  • Allowed members can send with /pishock shock, vibrate or beep, or from the buttons /pishock card posts.
  • Turn on /pishock penalties if you want a zap for a wrong quiz answer or a roast that lands on you.
  • /pishock stop pauses everything right away, and /pishock unlink removes it and its key.

Support tickets

Need help with one of our products? Run /ticket create with your question in the help channel, and the bot opens a private channel just for you and the VixForge team. Close it with /ticket close when you're sorted.

Keeping the server safe

The bot watches for trouble around the clock and deals with it before most people ever see it.

  • Scam images and links. Known scam pics, stickers and links are removed, even when they're forwarded, and the account that posted them is banned.
  • Spam. Mass pings, the same message posted across channels, and message floods are removed, and the sender is banned or timed out.
  • Raids. When a lot of accounts join at once, the bot goes into raid mode for a while, removes new joiners and tells staff.
  • New accounts. Staff get a heads up when a brand-new account joins.
  • Ghost pings. If someone pings you and deletes it, the bot says who it was.
  • Discord AutoMod. Staff get an alert when Discord's own AutoMod blocks a message.
  • Our server only. The bot works in the VixForge server and leaves any other server it's added to.

Staff tools

Each command shows only to staff with the matching Discord permission, and the bot won't act on anyone whose role is as high as yours.

  • Moderation. /mod warn, timeout, kick, ban and unban, each with a reason the member gets by DM. /mod history shows a member's warnings and actions from the last 90 days, and /mod unwarn removes a warning.
  • Channels. /mod purge deletes recent messages (all, or one member's), /mod slowmode slows a channel down, and /mod lock and /mod unlock close and reopen it.
  • Mod log. Every staff action is posted to a staff channel, set with /mod logchannel.
  • Server log. /serverlog posts deleted and edited messages, joins, leaves, bans and role changes to a staff-only channel. Each part can be switched off, busy channels can be left out, and posts are removed after 90 days.
  • Events. /event create plans an event in a voice channel or anywhere else, with a cover image. /event setup picks the announcement channel, a role to ping and when reminders go out, and events can start and end on time by themselves.
  • Giveaways. /giveaway start posts a giveaway with an Enter button, a timer, any number of winners and an optional role needed to enter. End it early, reroll or cancel it any time.
  • Posting. /announce posts a formatted announcement as the bot, with an optional role ping and image, and /poll starts a Discord poll.
  • Patch notes. The bot posts each new version of itself in the patch notes channel. /patchnotes previews or re-posts any version.
  • Behind The Forge. /forge post posts a Behind The Forge post one message per part: release notes and their TLDRs go to the patch notes channel, in-development notes to the Behind The Forge channel and news to the news channel, or to any channel you pick. /forge list shows what's ready, and /forge preview shows a post exactly as it will go out.
  • /ambient turns replies without an @, emoji reactions, greetings and the zoomies on or off, for the whole server or one channel.
  • /presence pins a status or activity, sets the bot's online state, or skips through its rotation.
  • /voiceadmin switches each voice feature on or off: the join and leave log, hellos, soundboard banter, member invites, go-live lines, hour marks, party lines, recaps and spoken lines. It also sets a join sound, a note when an empty channel opens, a chime when a release ships, and the bot's speaking voice and speed.
  • Records. The bot keeps the server's audit log and every Discord AutoMod hit for 90 days, longer than Discord's own 45-day audit log.
  • /spicyadmin sets which supporter roles can use the 18+ den.
  • Adventure submissions come to staff for review, and staff can reload or take an adventure offline.
