Upcoming Changes

What is coming in the next release, written as it stands today. Nothing here has shipped yet, so any of it may still change before it does. Everything already released is on the Shader Changelogs page.

VixenWear / Latex Ultra 5.10.0 - Unreleased

RGBA Color Masking, two more colour layers, Viewer Context by time of day, and Ink Weight that follows point lights and Light Volumes.

Added

  • Time of Day in every Viewer Context part: show a part only between two hours, read from the clock of each viewer, so everyone sees their own evening or morning at the same moment. From later than To runs past midnight, and Softness fades it over the hours you choose. It starts off, so nothing changes until you turn it up.
  • Viewer Context Show has three new choices: Only in Screenshots, Never in Screenshots and Only in Handheld Camera, so a saved photo and the live camera view can look different.
  • RGBA Color Masking under BASE: recolour up to four parts of the material from the red, green, blue and alpha of one mask. Each part has its own colour, texture, blend mode and glow. It works like the colour settings of Poiyomi's RGBA Color Masking and uses the same property names, so a Poiyomi material you switch over keeps them.
  • Second and Third Color Layers under BASE: lay up to two more colours or textures over the Albedo, each with its own UV, mask, blend, sides, distance fade and AudioLink. They work like Main Color 2nd and 3rd in lilToon and use the same property names, so a lilToon material you switch over keeps them.

Changed

  • Sharpness in Glitter Settings now also sets how fine the flakes of a layer with Sparkle on View are, on the surface and Above it. Raise it for smaller, tighter twinkles and lower it for broader, softer ones. At 90, the default, a flake is exactly Flake Size, so a material left at 90 looks the same. A material with another value shows smaller or bigger View flakes than before: set Sharpness to 90 to get the old flake size back.

Fixed

  • Ink Weight follows point lights and Light Volumes, not only the main light. A lamp or a Light Volume point light next to you used to leave the line weight unchanged. The weight now comes from every light the outline can see: the main light, Unity's vertex point lights, light probes, and Light Volumes with their point lights, following your Light Volumes settings. A point light that Unity draws per pixel still cannot change it, because the outline is drawn in one pass. Set that light's Render Mode to Not Important to include it.

VixForge Editor 2.11.0 - Unreleased

Switching a material onto a VixForge shader keeps its VRChat fallback.

Fixed

  • Switching a cutout or transparent material onto a VixForge shader keeps the matching VRChat Fallback Shader. Since VRChat SDK 3.10.6 it went back to the opaque fallback after the switch, so anyone with your shaders hidden saw that part solid.
  • Add Director Preview Rig keeps an AudioLink that is already in your scene working. It used to point that AudioLink at a new, empty Audio Source of its own, so the AudioLinkAvatar prefab's YTDLP player stopped reaching it and the Director heard nothing. The rig now listens to the AudioLink's own input (AudioLinkInput in the prefab) and changes nothing on it. If that AudioLink has no Audio Source yet, the rig uses the one its video player plays into.
  • Locked materials no longer carry effects that can never turn on. When a material marked one setting as Animated, locking kept every effect whose on/off check mentioned that setting, even when another setting had already switched the effect off. The most common case was Kinetic Shards: with AudioLink animated, two kinetic passes stayed in the locked shader while Kinetics was off, which made the upload bigger. Locking now removes an effect whenever it stays off whatever the animated setting does. Everything that is kept draws exactly as before. Re-lock your avatar to get the smaller upload.
