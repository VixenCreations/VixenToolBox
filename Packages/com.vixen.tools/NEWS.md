# What's New In 2.21

**VixenMMD gets your avatar dancing in MMD worlds. It builds the face shapes those worlds animate from shapes your avatar already has.**

## VixenMMD

Open it from `VixenTools/Avatars/VixenMMD`, or add it with **Add Component > VixenTools > VixenMMD**. It needs NDMF, which comes with Modular Avatar. It runs when you upload or enter Play Mode, so your avatar files never change.

* **Auto-fill** sets up most MMD morphs from your face tracking shapes and visemes. Press **Guess Empty Ones**, then **Preview Changes** and **Apply**.
* **Morphs** lists every MMD name, and lets you build each one from one or more of your shapes. Every list has a search box.
* **Dance Check** shows which shapes a dance's `.vmd` file uses and which ones your avatar is missing.
* **Convert Make It MMD Setup** moves an avatar's Make It MMD settings into VixenMMD in one click.

## Also New Since 2.20

* **PhysBone Blueprints keep colliders, ignore lists and root transforms.** Save your blueprints again to keep them.
* **Auto-Rig leaves meshes that are skinned to several bones as they were.**
* **The World Engine no longer leaves Unity's importing paused**, and **Live Surface Snapping** keeps a prefab's new position.
